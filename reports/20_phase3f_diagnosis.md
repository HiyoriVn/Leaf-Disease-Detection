# 20 — Phase 3F pipeline diagnosis and controlled plan (29/10/2026)

## Current training pipeline, verified from code/config

- **Data:** frozen Cassava manifest; labels ordered CBB, CBSD, CGM, CMD, Healthy. Counts are train 14,978 / validation 3,210 / internal test 3,209. Model selection is validation macro-F1; training reads train and validation samples, not internal-test images.
- **Baseline B3 input:** PIL RGB → float32 array → OpenCV cubic resize to 343×343 **without preserving source aspect ratio** → training augmentation → center crop 300×300 → scale to [0,1] → ImageNet mean/std. The crop retains 300/343 = 87.5% of the resized width/height; about 23.4% of the resized square's area is outside the crop. The square resize itself may distort non-square source images.
- **Existing training augmentation:** independent horizontal and vertical flips at p=0.5, random 0/90/180/270° rotation, then contrast and brightness multipliers sampled from [0.9, 1.1]. Current crop is centered after augmentation. Validation has no augmentation and uses the same resize/center crop.
- **B3 schedule:** ImageNet `efficientnet_b3.ra2_in1k`, 3 head-only epochs at LR 0.001; then by default `run_phase3e.ps1` fine-tunes `conv_head`, `classifier` and the last two backbone blocks for up to 10 epochs at LR 0.00003. AdamW, weight decay 0.0001, batch 16, AMP on CUDA, BatchNorm running-stat updates held in eval mode, early stopping patience 3. There is **no LR scheduler** in the Phase 3E configs. Checkpoint selection uses validation macro-F1.
- **Imbalance/loss:** baseline uses standard CE; V2 uses weighted CE with `(N/(K*n_class))**0.5`; V3 uses standard CE with label smoothing 0.05. The code supports both; do not describe weighting or smoothing as absent.

## Compute evidence and ceiling

The local measured GTX 1650 Max-Q MobileNet run recorded 1,358.46 s for its eight epochs, with validation macro-F1 peaking at epoch 6 (0.7044) and lower at epochs 7–8 (0.6839/0.6849), while training macro-F1 rose to 0.7381 by epoch 8. This is evidence of late overfit in that MobileNet run, **not a B3 or RTX 3060 timing estimate**. The B3 V2/V3 training logs were not supplied, so trustworthy B3 full-run duration estimates are unavailable. Record a timed PC pilot/first epoch, then estimate each run from its own observed epoch duration; do not extrapolate from the MobileNet laptop run.

V3's reported 83.55% implies about 2,682 correct of 3,210. At least 2,825 correct are required for 88%, and 2,889 for 90%. The target would require about 143–207 additional correct predictions; current evidence cannot say whether this is feasible. Validation is same-source and repeatedly used for selection; any reported gain needs a final locked internal-test evaluation and later independent field data for external claims.

## Method safeguards

Keep baseline, V2 and V3 checkpoints/configs; no Streamlit default change. Run only one controlled full experiment at a time after Phase 3E CSV comparison. Use unique run names. Pilot only checks the path, VRAM and code. Freeze labels, manifest, and internal test. Export `run.json`, `epochs.jsonl`, checkpoint SHA, per-class reports and predictions from every PC run. Stop the Phase 3F sequence if validation performance does not justify more tuning; avoid an open-ended sweep.
